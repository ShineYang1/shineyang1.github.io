### <span style="font-size: 0.8rem; color: #777; font-weight: lighter;">3rd Nov, 2026</span>


<br>

A warm winter,<br>
rainy summer,<br>
snowy nights,<br>
and green butterflies. <br>
<br>
Lament. <br>
For life is going to end.<br>
